# Ideal customer profile and qualification

The Qualifier scores every lead here. The point is not to be picky for its own sake, it is to
spend Lauren's time on the conversations most likely to become good work.

## Who The Pearson Co. is for

Founders and operators of growing businesses who have outgrown their sales setup and feel the
cost of it: leads slipping, forecasts they cannot trust, a CRM the team avoids, processes that
live in people's heads. They want a practical fix from someone who has run revenue operations,
not a slide deck.

### Fit signals (the more, the stronger)

- **Company stage:** post-revenue, roughly 5 to 200 people. Scaling, not pre-launch.
- **Sector fit:** B2B services, SaaS, and hospitality technology (Lauren's specialism). Any
  business with a real sales pipeline qualifies; hospitality tech and CRM/RevOps are the sweet spot.
- **Geography:** UAE and the wider GCC first, then UK and remote-friendly clients globally.
- **Role of the enquirer:** founder, CEO, commercial or revenue lead, head of sales or operations.
  Someone who owns the number or the system.
- **Named pain:** CRM adoption, pipeline and forecasting, process and SOPs, funnel conversion,
  or market entry. These map to the service pages.

### Out of profile (decline, kindly)

- Pre-revenue idea-stage startups with no pipeline yet.
- Pure B2C with no sales process to fix.
- Recruiters, agencies pitching Lauren, affiliate and SEO spam, generic partnership requests.
- Anyone asking for free ongoing work or a "quick call to pick your brain" with no fit.

## Scoring

Two axes, each scored, combined into a grade.

### Fit (0 to 5)
- +2 company in the right stage (5 to 200, post-revenue)
- +1 sector is B2B services, SaaS or hospitality tech
- +1 enquirer owns the number or the system (founder, commercial/ops lead)
- +1 geography is GCC or an established remote-friendly market

### Intent (0 to 5)
- +2 completed the scorecard, or sent a specific enquiry describing a real problem
- +1 scorecard band is "Money is slipping" or "Serious leakage" (a problem worth solving)
- +1 mentions a timeline, a trigger event, or budget
- +1 asked to talk, or requested the breakdown / a call

### Grade

| Grade | Rule | Handling |
|------|------|----------|
| **A** | Fit >= 4 and Intent >= 4 | Pursue now. Task HIGH. Scribe drafts response + call invite. Aim to book within 3 working days. |
| **B** | Fit >= 3 and Intent >= 3 | Pursue. Task MEDIUM. Scribe drafts response with a soft call offer. |
| **C** | Fit >= 2, any intent | Nurture. Task LOW. Draft a helpful reply, no pressure. Keep warm. |
| **D** | Fit < 2 or clearly out of profile | Decline kindly or ignore spam. No deal created. Note the reason. |

## Notes

- A weak scorecard result (band "Tight funnel") is not a disqualifier. It can mean a sharp
  operator who will value a second opinion. Read the message and the role, not just the number.
- When fit is unclear, grade one level down and let the discovery call decide.
- The grade sets effort and priority, never tone. Every human, including a D, gets a courteous
  reply if they wrote in person.
