# Outreach sequences: all drafts, never auto-sent

The Scribe writes from these into Gmail **drafts**. Lauren reads, edits, and sends. Merge
fields in `{{...}}`. Every draft follows house style: British English, no em dashes, no banned
words, straight quotes, warm and specific. Keep them short. A real person wrote these, so they
should read that way, not like a template with the blanks filled in.

Subject lines matter more than length. Reference the prospect's own result where you can.

---

## Rules for the Scribe

- Fill every merge field with something real. If a field cannot be filled truthfully, cut the
  sentence rather than leave a placeholder or invent a figure.
- One clear ask per message.
- Match the grade: A and B get the call offer up front, C gets value first and a soft door.
- Never state a cost, a saving, or a client name that has not been given to you by Lauren.
- Sign as Lauren. Save as a draft. Log that the draft exists in the deal's Task. Do not send.
