Common questions.

How is this different from a business coach?

A coach works on the leader: mindset, decisions, habits. I work on the commercial systems that produce revenue: sales process, CRM, pipeline and go-to-market. The output is operational, not personal.

What size of business do you work with?

Typically founder-led B2B businesses past product-market fit that have hit a ceiling, often between roughly £500k and £10m in revenue. That ceiling is usually a process or go-to-market problem, which is where I add the most direct value.

How long does an engagement take?

The diagnostic runs over the first few weeks. The fix-and-build phase usually spans a couple of months, reviewed fortnightly, then tapers as your team takes it over. It is shaped around what you can realistically absorb.
