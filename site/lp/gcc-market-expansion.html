Common questions.

Is this advice, or do you actually sell?

I sell. This is fractional sales support: I represent you in-market, run the meetings and work the pipeline on your behalf. The diagnostic and strategy stages exist to make that selling effective, not as the deliverable in themselves.

What happens at the end of the engagement?

Your call. We part ways with the market proven and the motion documented, or I help you hire and onboard your first in-market salesperson, or I keep selling for you on a tailored monthly retainer. Nothing is locked in.

Should we start with the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

For most technology and SaaS businesses, the UAE is the right first market: lower barriers to foreign business formation and a concentration of regional decision-makers that makes relationships easier to build. Saudi Arabia is usually the right second market, not the first.
