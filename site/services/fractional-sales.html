Fractional SaaS Sales

Fractional sales for SaaS companies in the GCC.

Lauren PearsonFounder, The Pearson Co.5 November 20266 min read

Fractional sales means I become your sales team on the ground in the GCC: opening doors, running the full sales cycle and closing deals, on a day rate plus commission. You get senior, local selling and real revenue, without the cost or risk of a full-time hire, until the market is proven.

Most SaaS companies do not lose the GCC because the product is wrong. They lose it because nobody is in the room. The region runs on relationships, trust and face-to-face meetings, and that is hard to do from London or Berlin, and just as hard when the founder is the only person selling between building, hiring and raising.

Fractional sales fixes that without a full-time hire. I come in as your senior seller in the market, carry a bag, and go and win customers.

Who this is for.

Two situations, same answer.

  • Coming into the region. You are winning at home, but the GCC plan is a future hire, a distributor who has gone quiet, or a trade-show trip where the leads went cold. A year on, a few nice coffees and no revenue.
  • Born in the region. You have built a great product, but no one owns sales. When you are busy building or fundraising, selling stops, and the business cannot grow past your calendar.

A seller, not a slide deck.

This is the difference that matters. I am not handing you a strategy and leaving. I am in the meetings, running the deals and closing them. You can read more on the different fractional sales models and what fractional sales leadership looks like for SaaS, but the short version is that I do the actual selling.

What that covers:

  • Prospecting and warm introductions across my GCC network
  • Discovery calls, demos and proposals
  • The full sales cycle, in person, to a signed contract
  • Qualified pipeline in your CRM, reported to you every week
  • A sales playbook you keep, so the motion outlasts the engagement

What you get

  • New customers, and a proven route to market
  • Real meetings with the people who actually buy
  • Weekly pipeline visibility, not a monthly guess
  • A documented playbook your future team can run

How it works.

We start with a free 20-minute GCC chat: where you are, where you want to go, and an honest view on whether I can help. If you are new to the region, I would usually recommend a GCC Market Entry Diagnostic first, so we prove the market before committing to a selling engagement. If you are ready to sell, I get on the ground and run the cycle, with weekly updates. Once the market is proven, I help you hire and hand over.

The commercial model.

A day rate, plus commission on new revenue. You pay for days on the ground, and I share the upside on what I bring in, so I am invested in results, not hours. Any pipeline you already have stays yours. It is a good fit if you have a SaaS product with paying customers, want GCC revenue in the next 6 to 12 months, and have a founder or sales leader involved. It is probably not a fit if you want commission-only, the product is not ready to sell, or you want a report rather than revenue.

If your sales engine itself needs work first, that is a different job: see revenue operations and hospitality tech and market entry, or read more on selling SaaS in the Middle East.

Common questions.

Do you only work with companies entering the GCC?

No. I also sell for SaaS companies born in the region that need someone dedicated to sales, not just the founder squeezing it in between building and fundraising.

Do you work commission-only?

No. It is a day rate plus commission on new revenue, so we are both committed. You pay for the days on the ground, and I share the upside on what I bring in.

What happens to our existing pipeline?

It stays yours. I focus on new opportunities, so commission is only ever paid on revenue I actually generate.

What if we want our own sales team later?

That is the goal. Once the market is proven, I help you hire and hand over the pipeline and the playbook, so your team picks up something that already works.
